###### A.6.1.1.2.2.6.1.3.1.12.3.2.4.3 - Swap Rate Limits [Core]

The swap rate limits are:

- AUSD: `maxAmount`: 1,000,000 AUSD, `slope`: 5,000,000 AUSD per day
- USDC: `maxAmount`: 1,000,000 USDC, `slope`: 5,000,000 USDC per day
- `maxSlippage`: 0.1%
